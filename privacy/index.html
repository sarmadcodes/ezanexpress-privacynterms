Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data EzanExpress collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. It applies to the EzanExpress mobile app and this website.

Identity documents. To use EzanExpress you must upload a passport or driving licence. This is sensitive information, so we describe it separately below. Please read Identity documents before you sign up.

Who we are

EzanExpress operates the EzanExpress app. For the purposes of UK data protection law we are the data controller for the information described below. You can reach us at ezanexpress@gmail.com.

Identity documents

We ask for a passport or driving licence because EzanExpress puts strangers in contact to hand over physical goods, often crossing a border. Verified identity is what makes that safe enough to offer.

  • Your document is used solely to verify that you are who you say you are, and that you are 18 or over.
  • It is reviewed by our team during account approval. It is never shown to other users and never published.
  • It is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • We keep it only while your account is active, and delete it when your account is deleted, unless we are legally required to retain it.
  • We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to train any automated system.

We do not use your personal data for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies and their apps or websites.

Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job.

  • Other users. When you agree a delivery, the other party sees your first name, your rating, and the address and contact details needed to complete that specific handover. They never see your identity document, your date of birth, or your password.
  • Stripe processes payments. Stripe receives your card details directly and acts as its own controller for them.
  • Geoapify provides address lookup. The text you type into the address field is sent to Geoapify to return suggestions.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, who store data on our behalf under contract.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where we are legally required to disclose, or where disclosure is necessary to investigate a serious safety incident.

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Identity documents: while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Transaction records: up to 7 years, because tax and accounting law requires it.
  • Safety reports and blocks: up to 3 years after an account closes, so that a banned user cannot simply register again.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • correct anything inaccurate
  • delete your data, subject to the retention periods above
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • transfer it to another provider in a portable format
  • withdraw consent, where we relied on consent

Email ezanexpress@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner at ico.org.uk.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time, under Profile, then Delete Account. You do not need to email us first. Deletion removes your profile, your identity document and your personal details, subject to the legal retention periods set out above.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never recoverable in plain text. Access to identity documents is restricted to staff who need it for verification. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the Information Commissioner of any breach affecting your rights, as the law requires.

Children

EzanExpress is not for anyone under 18. We verify age from the identity document you provide. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18 we close it and delete the data.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or on standard contractual clauses approved for UK transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the date at the top of this page and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
