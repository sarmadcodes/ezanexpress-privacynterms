Support

Last updated 24 October 2026

Email ezanexpress@gmail.com and we will reply within two working days. For anything involving safety, write Urgent in the subject line.

If you are in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services first. Tell us afterwards so we can act on the account.

Account verification

New accounts are reviewed before they are approved, which usually takes one working day. If your account is still pending after that, check that the photograph of your passport or driving licence is sharp, uncropped, and that all four corners are visible. You can replace the document from the signup screen and we will review it again.

Reporting a user

Open the person profile and choose Report. Tell us what happened and include the delivery reference if there is one. You can also block a user, which stops them contacting you or seeing your requests. We investigate every report, and we will tell you the outcome.

Report immediately if anyone asks you to carry a sealed package, refuses to let you inspect the contents, or offers a payment outside the app.

Payments

Payments are held until delivery is confirmed, then released to the traveller. If a delivery did not happen, or the parcel arrived damaged, email us within 14 days with the delivery reference and photographs. Refunds return to the original payment method and can take up to 10 working days to appear.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete Account. You do not need to contact us. This removes your profile, your identity document and your personal details. Transaction records are kept for the period required by tax law, as set out in the Privacy Policy.

Requesting your data

Email us to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to object to how it is used. We respond within one month. See the Privacy Policy for the full list of your rights.
