What EzanExpress is

EzanExpress is a marketplace. It introduces people who want to send a parcel to travellers who have spare luggage capacity on a flight they have already booked. We are not a courier, a freight forwarder, or a carrier. We do not take possession of any parcel, and we do not transport anything ourselves. The agreement to carry a parcel is between the sender and the traveller.
